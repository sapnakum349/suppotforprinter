Printer Setup Guide

Follow these basic steps to get started.

Step 1: Unpack Your Printer

Remove packaging materials and place the printer on a flat surface.

Step 2: Connect Power

Connect the power cable and switch on your printer.

Step 3: Install Printer Drivers

Download compatible drivers from the printer manufacturer's official website.

Step 4: Connect Your Printer

Connect using USB or follow your printer's Wi-Fi setup instructions.

Step 5: Print a Test Page

Check printer settings and print a test page to verify installation.
